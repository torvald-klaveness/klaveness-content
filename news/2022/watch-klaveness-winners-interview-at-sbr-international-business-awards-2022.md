In May 2022, Torvald Klaveness was recognized at the SBR International Business Awards, taking home the winner’s trophy in the category for ‘Shipping’. The awards program, presented by *Singapore Business Review*, gives recognition to the outstanding projects of foreign companies in Singapore.

Simon Hyett, Contributing Editor at Singapore Business Review interviews Michael Jørgensen, SVP, Head of Dry Bulk to learn how Klaveness changed the status quo for the traditional processes in the shipping industry with the Market Manager initiative.

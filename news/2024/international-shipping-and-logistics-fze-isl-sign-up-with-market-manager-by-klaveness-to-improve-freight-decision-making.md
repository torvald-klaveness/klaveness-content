Oslo, 16 April 2024: International Shipping and Logistics, FZE (ISL) and Market Manager by Klaveness have agreed a pilot deal that sees ISL onboard the Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform to improve freight and vessel decisions.

ISL, a valued existing customer of Klaveness and currently trading a vessel in the Baumarine by MaruKlav shipping pool, have signed on to adopt the ‘Freight Optimizer’ and ‘Pre-Vetting’ modules offered by the Market Manager platform.

Capt. Som Sekhar Mishra, Director and Chief Executive Officer, comments: “We see the Market Manager platform by Klaveness as a significant step forward in our digitalization journey. The tools offer us great potential to create more value with high quality data backing our vessel choices and freight decisions.”

Michael Jørgensen, Head of Klaveness Dry Bulk, adds: “It’s great to have ISL onboard as we see Market Manager creating real value for our clients. Our mission is to provide insights that are accurate and actionable, supporting our clients’ decision making. We look forward to incorporating ISL’s feedback in our development process to ensure that the platform stays relevant”.

About ISL

International Shipping & Logistics (ISL) FZE, UAE, is based in Jebel Ali Free Zone and offers services related to operating ships carrying dry cargo for its various clients across geographies. ISL's span of activities in terms of port calls ranges from the Baltic and the Black Sea ports in Europe to Indian, Thai and Chinese ports in Asia, including all major ports in the Red Sea and the Persian Gulf.

About Market Manager by Klaveness

A Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform crafted by Klaveness Chartering, incorporating extensive commercial expertise, and backed by our best-in-class research team. The platform incorporates three core modules Pre-Vetting, Port Predictor and Freight Optimizer, enabling its customers to drive new value to the topline and make better informed decisions to navigate shipping markets.

Lasse Kristoffersen, who has pioneered Klaveness’ transformation since 2011 as President and CEO, has decided to depart the company June 1st, 2022

- The Board of Directors will immediately begin the process of identifying a new CEO to ensure a smooth transition
- Lasse defined a clear company vision, revitalized the values the company has stood for these 75 years, and created a legacy that will define us for many years to come, says Chairman Trond Harald Klaveness

**Oslo, 29th November 2021:** Torvald Klaveness today announces that President and CEO Lasse Kristoffersen, who has pioneered the company’s transformation since 2011, has decided to depart the company from June 1st, 2022 and begins as CEO of Wallenius Wilhelmsen. The Board of Directors will immediately begin the process of identifying a new CEO to ensure a smooth transition.

Under Kristoffersen’s leadership, Klaveness was steered into a new era of strategic growth fueled by passionate digitalization and decarbonization ambitions. At the same time, Kristoffersen created a robust organization built on diversifying the product and service portfolio, restructuring the Klaveness business units, and creating a strong company culture built on a clear vision and values.

“I wish to express my sincere gratitude to Lasse for his time as our CEO, under his leadership the company has made many landmark achievements which are reflected in our performance these past 10 years, our current position, and our strategic direction going forward,” says Chairman Trond Harald Klaveness.

“I am honored to have been given the trust to run Torvald Klaveness for more than a decade. It has been an exciting and rewarding period and I am proud that the company today is in a very strong position and with a prosperous future,” says Lasse Kristoffersen.

Trond Harald Klaveness adds, “Lasse defined a clear company vision, revitalized the values the company has stood for these 75 years, and created a legacy that will define us for many years to come.”

## About Klaveness

Torvald Klaveness is a pioneering shipping company with the vision to improve the nature of shipping and the ambition to make seaborn supply chains resilient, decarbonized, and cost-effective. The group today consists of five business units; Through Klaveness Combination Carriers, the company takes an active part in the transition to low-carbon shipping with the unique concept of combination carriers. Our Dry Bulk unit is a leading operator and service provider to the global dry bulk industry and manages the world’s largest Panamax Pool. Klaveness Ship Management manages the owned vessels from an environmental, technical, and commercial standpoint. Klaveness Digital has developed the world’s leading platform for managing seaborne supply chains. Our newest venture Klaveness ZeroLab is developing several initiatives to make low-and zero emission options available to our customers.

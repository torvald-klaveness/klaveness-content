**New whitepaper available for download**

With the first full cycles of FuelEU and EU ETS underway, emissions reporting has become part of everyday operations. But for many charterers and cargo owners, the process is still shaped by fragmented data, manual workflows, and growing regulatory pressure — making it harder than it should be to get to the numbers.

This in-depth whitepaper explores the realities of emissions reporting today, shares practical examples of where time and value are lost, and explains how forward-thinking teams are turning emissions data into something they can actually use.

Inside, you’ll learn:

- Why emissions data is often available but still difficult to trust, and how fragmented workflows are slowing teams down.
- How manual validation, disconnected systems, and delayed inputs are creating hidden operational costs across reporting cycles.
- How charterers and cargo owners are taking practical steps to move from time-consuming reporting processes to structured, decision-ready data.

Download your copy to learn how smarter emissions data management can save time, reduce friction, and support better decisions across your portfolio.

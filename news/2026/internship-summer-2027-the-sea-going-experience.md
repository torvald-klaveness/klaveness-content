We are looking for an active student with maritime and shipping interests for an exciting, challenging and different summer internship for the summer of 2027.

The internship will start at the head office in Oslo with an introduction to the Torvald Klaveness group, Klaveness Combination Carriers (KCC) and Klaveness Ship Management by OSM Thome (KSM), and preparations for sailing and life onboard.

The next 6-10 weeks will be on board one of KCC’s globally trading combination carriers. The student will receive a defined training program spanning over all the departments and systems when onboard, and the candidate will also be involved in specific projects from the HQ in Oslo to be followed up from the vessel. The specific vessel will be selected based on the fleets’ schedule and trading pattern.

The right candidate must be genuinely interested in learning from practical experience, show interest and respect for other cultures, and in general be positive and open to challenges underway. The candidate must have completed the 2nd year or higher of an integrated master program in engineering, alternatively have succeeded in acquiring a bachelor’s degree in engineering and is entering a Master program.

Post your written application including a 1-minute video, as well as your latest grades, **by 31 October 2026 by using this link:**  [https://nuu.no/torvald\_klaveness/stilling/24882](https://nuu.no/torvald_klaveness/stilling/24882)

**About Torvald Klaveness:**

Torvald Klaveness is a pioneering shipping group with a vision to improve the nature of shipping.  Through its portfolio companies, Klaveness helps customers move cargo more efficiently by combining maritime expertise, innovative shipping solutions, and digital capabilities. Together, these solutions increase trading efficiency and support more resilient, decarbonized, and cost-effective seaborne supply chains.

**Klaveness Holding** oversees the group’s ownership interests and investments. **Klaveness Combination Carriers,** listed on the Oslo Stock Exchange, owns and operates a fleet of combination carriers, **Klaveness Dry Bulk** is a leading global dry bulk operator and manager of the world’s largest Panamax pool, and **Klaveness Digital** develops digital solutions for seaborne supply chains and chartering decision support.

**Klaveness Ship Management by OSM Thome** manages the owned vessels from an environmental, technical, and commercial standpoint.

With 80 years of experience, Klaveness operates approximately 75 vessels and employs around 155 people across offices in Oslo, Singapore, Manila, Dubai and Tokyo.
